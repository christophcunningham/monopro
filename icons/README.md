# Embedded icons

Only SVGs referenced by the application are included here.

- Most glyphs are Phosphor Icons, under the MIT license. The upstream notice is
  preserved in `LICENSE-Phosphor.txt`.
- `square-arrow-out-up-right.svg` and `square-arrow-out-down-left.svg` are Lucide
  glyphs. The upstream license, including its Feather attribution, is preserved
  in `LICENSE-Lucide.txt`.
- `mask.svg`, `gradient-linear.svg` and `gradient-radial.svg` were drawn for
  monopro, on Phosphor's 256 grid with its 16-unit stroke so they sit with
  `circle.svg` and `square.svg` (Phosphor) as one set of shape icons.

Upstream license sources (retrieved 2026-09-15):

- https://github.com/phosphor-icons/core/blob/main/LICENSE
- https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/main/LICENSE

Application-specific geometry and styling may differ from upstream glyphs.
